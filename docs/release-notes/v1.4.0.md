# PDFCS 1.4.0

**Pages that look typeset, a camera that tells you how to hold it, and your photos kept.**

This release comes from "001J STD IPC (searchable)", the 93-page IPC J-STD-001J scan converted again with 1.3.0. That copy still had several problems:
- typefaces, sizes and baselines changed within a line;
- capitals were misread ("ReqUIrements", "COMPly");
- some pages shrank into their top corner.

All of these are fixed here.

### Highlights
- **Live alignment guide.** The camera shows a page-shaped target with a centre cross, and outlines the page it sees:
  - amber while you adjust;
  - red when part of the page is outside the picture;
  - green when it's ready.

  One instruction at a time tells you what to fix: fit the whole page in, move closer, centre it, hold the phone flat, line it up, hold still. Turn on **Shoot automatically when lined up** in Settings and the camera takes each page by itself.
- **Your photos are kept.** Turn on **Save original photos** in Settings and pick a folder. Every scan's photos are copied there before conversion, one folder per scan ("Page 001.jpg"…). Use **Library → Add → Photo folder to searchable PDF** to convert them again later.
- **One paper size, one layout.** Pages are laid out together once the whole document is read:
  - one standard paper size;
  - a page photographed whole sits exactly as printed;
  - a badly cropped page gets the document's text size and margins.

  Pages no longer shrink into a corner.
- **Pages keep their real shape**, even when photographed at a slant. A printed band along the page's edge (a coloured footer) is no longer cut off.
- **Clean, even text.**
  - One typeface for the whole document, and one size and baseline per line.
  - Line starts line up.
  - No stray bold words, and correct capitals.
  - Units and values such as MHz, pF and VRM=5.25V are left exactly as read.
- **Fewer pieces kept as pictures.** Words the reader is sure of are typeset. Numbers still have to match the page closely, so a date or dimension is never printed wrong. A word kept as its original pixels moves with its line and is cleaned to black on white.
- **"Original photos" PDFs** now also use one paper size for the whole document.

### Fixed
- Saving over an existing file (Save to device, exports) could leave a corrupt file.
- A failed import left a broken copy in the library.
- Word (.docx) exports opened on a blank page.
- Optimize kept old undo copies without limit.

### Measured
Converted end to end on an emulator:
- **ST USBLC6-2 datasheet photos:**
  - **86%** of words word-exact over three pages (1.3.0: 81%), with page 1 at 94%;
  - the coloured footer band is kept;
  - on A4 paper with even margins.
- **IPC J-STD-001J pages** (from the 1.3.0 scan):
  - every page on Letter with even margins;
  - nearly all body text typeset in one typeface on one baseline;
  - no misread number printed.
- **Page finding on 40 photographed scenes:** unchanged (37 within 1.5%). The live guide found the page in 39 of 40 preview frames.

### Install
Download the APK for your phone below and open it:
- `PDFCS-v1.4.0-arm64-v8a.apk`: almost every phone from the last several years. If unsure, take this one.
- `PDFCS-v1.4.0-armeabi-v7a.apk`: older 32-bit phones.
- `PDFCS-v1.4.0-universal.apk`: works everywhere, but is larger.

It updates PDFCS 1.3.0 in place, and your library is kept. For the best IPC document, scan the pages again with the guide and convert them. Words kept as pixels in an old PDF keep its flaws.

### Requirements
Android 8.0 or newer. The English OCR model is built in. Other languages can be downloaded in Settings.

### Known limitations
- Words the reader can't verify still keep their original pixels. If those pixels are poor (a blurred or re-converted copy), they show it.
- The live guide can't see a page that fills the whole picture. Keep a little background around it.
- Not yet tested on a real phone camera, only on the emulator's.
