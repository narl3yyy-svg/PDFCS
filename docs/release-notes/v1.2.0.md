# PDFCS 1.2.0

**Clean pages, no background, OCR by itself.**

This release makes a photographed page come out like a PDF made on a computer: the desk or tablecloth
around it is gone, the page is straight, and every line of text is level and flush with the margins.
Imports now become searchable PDFs automatically.

### Highlights
- **The background disappears.** PDFCS checks every possible outline of the sheet against what a page looks like: one paper colour inside, a real edge on every side, and a different scene outside. The desk, the cloth and the next page are cropped away. Coloured bands printed along the page's edge, like a datasheet's footer, are kept.
- **Straight pages.** The page is levelled from its text lines to 0.05°. If the line starts still drift sideways, the columns are stood upright.
- **Aligned text.** Each printed line sits on one baseline at one size, body text has one size, and lines are flush with the margins. Before, a slightly tilted photo could leave words stepping up and down.
- **Automatic OCR on import** (on by default):
  - Import a scanned PDF and it is converted to a clean, searchable PDF in the background, keeping its name.
  - Pick photos with *Images to searchable PDF* and each page is found, cropped and converted. There is no review step.
  - PDFs that already have text are imported as they are.
  - Turn this off in Settings → *OCR imports automatically*.
- **"Make searchable"** in the library and the reader now runs directly in the background.

### Measured
Simulated hand-held photos on wood, light and white desks, dark cloth and a checked tablecloth were run end to end on an emulator:
- **Book pages:** 99.2–100% of words word-exact (9 pages, 5 backgrounds).
- **ST USBLC6-2 datasheet:** 96% on page 1 and 82% over three pages (tables and graph labels included). The previous release scored 80%.
- **Hold-out scenes (40):** all pages level, 39 upright, and no background left on any of the 20 book pages.

### Fixes
- Opening a PDF from another app (Files, Share) could cancel its own import.
- The automatic crop could cut off a band printed along the page's edge.
- Pages taken from a PDF could be cropped to a printed band.
- Stripes of a tablecloth along the edge no longer turn into "|" characters or stray marks.

### Install
Download `PDFCS-v1.2.0.apk` below and open it on your phone. It updates PDFCS 1.1.0 in place, and your library is kept.

### Requirements
Android 8.0 or newer. The English OCR model is built in. Other languages can be downloaded in Settings.

### Known limitations
- Digital-text pages support Latin, Greek and Cyrillic scripts. For other scripts, use the *Original photos* output.
- Subscripts, very small print and graph labels are often kept as original pixels rather than text.
- A page photographed with a corner outside the picture can keep a thin sliver of background along that edge.
